## Nutrition, quality and safety references

- [FEDIAF nutritional guidelines](https://europeanpetfood.org/self-regulation/nutritional-guidelines/): the provider links the 2025 cats/dogs edition. Use species and life stage when defining any substitution constraints.
- [FEDIAF safety guidance](https://europeanpetfood.org/self-regulation/safety/): handling, manufacturing and storage context.
- [EFSA mycotoxins](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/topic/mycotoxins): scientific context for climate-sensitive ingredient-quality risks.

Farm-animal energy/digestibility values in general feed tables must not be treated as dog/cat values. Weather-derived risk is not a measured contamination result. Ingredient substitutions require a separate nutritional assessment; the project can compare sourcing alternatives without asserting that a complete food is safe or balanced.

## Information needed for a recipe scenario

Record ingredient mass shares, delivered form, moisture, source zones, crop-to-ingredient conversion yield, processing/transport assumptions, co-product allocation and impact-factor units. Poultry protein also needs upstream feed and poultry-production assumptions.

For comparable mass-based factors, ingredient impact per kg of mixture can be estimated as the sum of each mass share multiplied by its compatible per-kg factor. Add any stages outside the factors' boundaries separately and avoid double counting. Label this as a scoped estimate unless the full product life cycle is covered.

Do not compare mixtures with different nutritional function or moisture content as if a kilogram were automatically equivalent. A local ingredient is not necessarily lower impact: quantify the relevant production and processing differences.
