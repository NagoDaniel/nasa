## Dataset citations

Use the provider's recommended citation for the **exact collection/version used**, including its DOI where supplied. Add your actual download date and describe the geographic/temporal subset. The catalogue review date is not the download date.

Record this for every input:

> Provider / authors. Dataset title. Product ID and version. DOI or stable provider URL. Observation period and geographic subset. Actual date accessed. Relevant transformations and QA policy.

Use source IDs from [sources.json](sources.json) in processed-data metadata, figure captions and application explanations. Preserve raw provider metadata alongside downloads. The full provider-link list is [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

## References versus data

- Reference pages document methodology or context; they are not proof that a dataset was downloaded or used.
- The challenge mirror is a secondary navigation source, not the authority for eligibility.
- The ingredient pages are generic composition references, not batch laboratory certificates.
- FEDIAF documents are linked rather than reproduced. Their copyright/use terms remain in force.
- Third-party datasets and the supplied event guide retain their original rights. This folder does not impose a new license on them.

Before submitting the project, list the actual NASA and other sources used, the relevant methods and any AI contribution as requested by the submission form. Keep claims about measured values, estimated quantities and hypothetical scenarios distinct.
